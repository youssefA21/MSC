Our mission and vision

Everything the club does starts from one idea: tech skills should not belong to one major.

MISSION

Empower students across disciplines to build tech communities, gain future skills with Microsoft tools, and solve real world problems together.

VISION

A campus where every student, from any faculty, can build tech skills and turn ideas into real impact.

Questions students ask

Who can join the club?

Students from any faculty. The club is built for people across disciplines, so you do not need a computer science background.

When does recruitment open?

Online recruitment is open now.

What will I learn?

Technical tracks cover AI, Data, Web, Cybersecurity, C#, Problem Solving, and Programming Fundamentals. Other teams build skills in HR, marketing, media, PR, and event management.

Do I need experience with Microsoft tools?

No. Sessions start from the basics and we teach the tools as you go.

Is the club part of Microsoft?

We are part of the Microsoft Student Clubs network in Egypt, run by students at SAMS.

Is there a fee to join?

No. Membership is free.

How do I contact the club?

Message us on Instagram at @microsoft.sams. We reply there.
